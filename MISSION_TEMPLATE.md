# Mission: <name>

*Copy this file into a new repository and fill every section. Unfilled sections block recognition.
Copy `DAYS.md`, `RETRO.md`, and `DATA_POLICY.md` from the movement's
[templates folder](https://github.com/onedayforhumanity/onedayforhumanity/tree/main/templates) beside it.*

## 1. The problem
Who suffers, how many, and how we know. Two paragraphs maximum. Cite sources.

## 2. What we will build, and what we won't
Concrete scope. Name the existing open projects we build on rather than duplicate.

## 3. Who is affected, and who leads
Council members from the affected community, with names and roles. They hold a blocking vote
on the mission's scope, its use of data, and any release to the people it serves. A block is
resolved by the Steering Circle within 30 days.

## 4. Steward
Name, organization, contact. Backup steward if any. A mission with no steward for 60 days is paused.

## 5. Definition of done
What "this mission succeeded" looks like in measurable terms, and what "we should stop" looks like.

## 6. Day-sized backlog
Link to the issue tracker. Confirm the `one-day` and `good-first-day` labels exist and at least
10 issues are groomed before launch. Every day given is one line in `DAYS.md`, started from the
movement's template.

## 7. Licenses
- Code: <OSI-approved license>
- Data and content: <open license>

## 8. Data, privacy, and devices
What data is touched, where it lives, who can access it, how consent is obtained, and how it is
de-identified. The floor is GDPR Article 9 treatment for all health data and HIPAA Security Rule
safeguards wherever protected health information is touched. State which medical-device rules
apply to anything the mission ships, and whether anything a dosing or treatment decision could
depend on is in scope. Link `DATA_POLICY.md`, started from the movement's template.

## 9. Digital Public Goods Standard checklist
- [ ] Relevance to an SDG
- [ ] Approved open license
- [ ] Clear ownership
- [ ] Platform independence
- [ ] Documentation
- [ ] Mechanism for extracting data
- [ ] Adherence to privacy and applicable laws
- [ ] Adherence to standards and best practices
- [ ] Do no harm by design

## 10. Metrics
Three numbers we will publish every quarter, and where they come from.

## 11. Maintenance share
What fraction of contributed days goes to maintaining existing work (default: one in four).

## 12. Retrospective log
Link to `RETRO.md`, started from the movement's template. Updated at least quarterly. Failures
included.
