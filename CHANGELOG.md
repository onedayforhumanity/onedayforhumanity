# Changelog

Every released version of the manifesto and the principles is recorded here, newest first.
People sign a version. The version they signed is recorded in [SIGNATORIES.md](SIGNATORIES.md).

## 0.2, 2026-09-06

The manifesto was split in two. [MANIFESTO.md](MANIFESTO.md) is now the short text people
sign: the opening, the six value pairs, and how to join. [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md) holds
the rules, merged from the six commitments and twelve principles of 0.1 into ten.

Wording changes, and why:

- "Nobody asked, and nobody built the door" was replaced with a sentence that names Pledge 1%
  and says what we do differently. Others have asked; the claim was not true.
- "This is the door", "We are the ones who do", and "enough to move the world" were cut. They
  were not promises anyone could check.
- "No one owns it" became "Everyone may use it, forever. The licenses we use cannot be
  revoked." Copyright always has an owner; what is permanent is the grant.
- "Held in trust by the movement" became a promise about the future plus the fact of today:
  held by the founder until a non-profit home exists.
- "Strictest applicable standard" for health data became a named floor: GDPR Article 9, HIPAA
  Security Rule safeguards where protected health information is touched, and a statement of
  which medical-device rules apply.
- "Can veto direction" became a defined blocking vote on scope, data use, and release, resolved
  by the Steering Circle within 30 days. The same text is now in GOVERNANCE.md and
  MISSION_TEMPLATE.md.
- "We stand on neutral ground ... we do not take sides" became "We are impartial", with a harm
  assessment before any conflict data is touched. A group that builds early-warning tools
  cannot promise not to take sides. It can promise to choose by need and to put the people it
  could harm first.
- "Renewed every year" now applies to organizations. Individuals are counted by the days they
  log.
- The pause rule now matches GOVERNANCE.md: a mission with no steward for 60 days is paused.
- A day counts when it ends with something "merged, reviewed, tested, or learned", matching
  CONTRIBUTING.md.
- The maintenance share is now countable: one in four of a mission's days by default, matching
  MISSION_TEMPLATE.md.
- The employer question is addressed for the first time: an employer's day is pledged with the
  employer's authorization of the license; your own time is your own.
- The value pairs are bare, as in the Agile Manifesto. The explanation of "continuity over
  intensity" moved to the principles.
- Who decides on changes before the Steering Circle exists is now stated.
- A naming norm was added under CC BY 4.0: copy it whole, and do not call a changed version the
  One Day for Humanity manifesto.

The research behind these changes is in [research/manifesto.md](research/manifesto.md).
