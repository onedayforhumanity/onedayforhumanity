# The One Day for Humanity Manifesto

*Version 0.2, a draft for public comment. What changed since 0.1 is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).*

---

Most of the world's technical talent spends every working hour on problems that pay.
Disease, hunger, war, exclusion: the problems that matter most are worked on by too few,
with too little, for too short a time.

Others have asked before us. Pledge 1% counts 20,000 companies, and the most common thing
they pledge is time. Most of that time is scattered and unreported. We pool it into missions
with a steward and a backlog, and we publish what came of it, wins and misses alike.

**One Day for Humanity** is a commitment by people and organizations to give a fixed,
recurring share of their working time to humanity's hardest problems, and to give the
results away.

---

## We value

- **Shipped** over promised
- **Open** over owned
- **Useful and unglamorous** over impressive and unused
- **Continuity** over intensity
- **The people affected** over the people building
- **Evidence** over applause

That is, while the things on the right have their place, we choose the things on the left.

The [principles](PRINCIPLES.md) say what we do about it: one day, counted and kept; open
output that cannot be taken back; missions with a steward, a backlog, and a definition of
done; failure published in the same font as progress.

## Join

- **Individuals:** sign by [adding one line to SIGNATORIES.md](https://github.com/onedayforhumanity/onedayforhumanity/edit/main/SIGNATORIES.md): your name, your cadence, and the version you signed. If you do not use git, [open an issue](https://github.com/onedayforhumanity/onedayforhumanity/issues/new?template=sign.yml) and we will add you.
- **Organizations:** read [PLEDGE.md](PLEDGE.md), publish your first pledge post, then sign the same way.
- **Everyone:** [nominate a mission](https://github.com/onedayforhumanity/onedayforhumanity/issues/new?template=mission-nomination.yml), or help groom the founding mission's backlog. When a mission is recognized, pick an issue labelled `one-day` and give your first day.
