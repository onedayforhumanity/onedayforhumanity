## Pinned post (280 characters at most; this is about 240)

People and organizations give one day, every week, month, quarter, or year, to humanity's
hardest problems, and give the results away. Counted, kept, reported in public. The manifesto
is a draft and open for comment. onedayforhumanity.org

## A first thread

1. One Day for Humanity is open for comment. Here is the idea in four posts.

2. The unit is one day. Weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly: the cadence is yours, the
   commitment is public, every cadence counts. Organizations pledge per person who opts in,
   and report once a year, wins and misses alike.

3. The time goes into missions: open-source work with a named steward, a backlog cut into
   day-sized tasks, and a definition of done. Everything built is a public good. Everyone may
   use it, forever.

4. Nothing is launched. The manifesto is a draft, the founding mission (open diabetes
   technology) is a proposal, and the first steward has not stepped forward. Read it, sign it,
   or argue with it: onedayforhumanity.org

## A note on tone

Same rules as everywhere else: no exclamation marks, no emoji, plain sentences. Reply to people
who disagree; the manifesto says argue with it, in the open.
