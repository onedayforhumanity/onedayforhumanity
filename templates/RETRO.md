# Retrospective log: <mission>

What worked, what did not, and what changed as a result. Newest entry first. Written at least
once a quarter, and whenever something fails. Failures go in the same place and the same font as
wins ([PRINCIPLES.md](https://github.com/onedayforhumanity/onedayforhumanity/blob/main/PRINCIPLES.md)).
This log is public, and the mission's quarterly report links to it.

## <year>, quarter <n>

**Period.** <start date> to <end date>. Days given: <N>, from `DAYS.md`, of which <M> were
maintenance.

**What worked.** What shipped, who it reached, and how we know. A paragraph or a short list.

**What did not.** What we tried, what happened, and what it cost. The same length as the section
above.

**What changed as a result.** The decision, who made it, and where it is recorded: an issue, a
pull request, or a council vote.

**Against the definition of done.** Closer, further, or unchanged, measured by the three numbers
in section 10 of the mission file. If "we should stop" is nearer than "we succeeded", say so
here.
