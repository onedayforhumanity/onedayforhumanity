# Contributing: how to give a day

## Before your first day (15 minutes)

1. Read [MANIFESTO.md](MANIFESTO.md) and [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md). If you agree, add
   yourself to [SIGNATORIES.md](SIGNATORIES.md), and say whether your days are your own time or
   your employer's (see [PLEDGE.md](PLEDGE.md)).
2. Pick a recognized mission, or help groom the founding mission's backlog while it seeks a
   steward. Read the mission's `README` and its definition of done (section 5 of its mission
   file).
3. Find an issue labelled `one-day` (or `good-first-day` if you are new).
   Every one of these is scoped to be finished in a single working day.
4. Comment "taking this" so nobody duplicates your work.

## During your day

- Work in the open: draft PRs early, ask questions in the issue.
- If the task turns out to be bigger than a day, split it. Open a new issue for the
  remainder and finish the part you can.
- Sign your commits with `git commit -s` (Developer Certificate of Origin).

## Ending your day

A day counts when it ends with at least one of:
- something **merged** (code, docs, data, design),
- something **reviewed** for someone else,
- something **tested** with a written result,
- something **learned** and written down in the mission's retrospective log.

Add one line to the mission's `DAYS.md`: date, name or organization, what you did. That line
is how individuals are counted.

## What we need besides code

Testing, documentation, translation, UX research, data cleaning, clinical or domain
review, community support, project management. Many `one-day` issues need no code at all.

## Pull requests

- One PR per issue. Small is good.
- Explain *who* the change helps, not just *what* it does.
- Maintainers aim to review within 7 days. If that passes, ping the steward.

## Safety and data

Never commit personal data, credentials, or patient records. Missions that handle health
data have their own `DATA_POLICY.md`. Read it before touching data.

## Code of conduct

We follow the Contributor Covenant. Be kind, assume good faith, and report problems to the
steward or to `conduct@`.
