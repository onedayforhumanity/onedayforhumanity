# Days given to <mission>

One line per day, newest at the bottom: the date, who gave the day, and what it ended with. A
day counts when it ends with something merged, reviewed, tested, or learned
([CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/onedayforhumanity/onedayforhumanity/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)).
A day that ended with none of those is not listed. Whether the day was your own time or your
employer's is recorded where you signed, not here.

The number of lines is the number of days; that is how individuals are counted. A line marked
maintenance counts toward the mission's share of days for keeping earlier work alive, one in four
by default
([PRINCIPLES.md](https://github.com/onedayforhumanity/onedayforhumanity/blob/main/PRINCIPLES.md)).
